# Unified Reporting

New-to-brand and Adelaide metrics are now available in Unified Reporting,
expanding the set of measurement dimensions advertisers can report on
alongside standard performance metrics.

In Unified Reporting, time dimension data retention has been extended to 24
months, allowing reporting queries to reach further back into historical
data than previously supported.
